- [C#](/framework/develop/authoring-csharp/), [F#](/framework/develop/authoring-fsharp/), and [out-of-process](/framework/develop/authoring-rpc/) plugins.
- [Testing](/framework/develop/testing/) and [troubleshooting](/framework/develop/troubleshooting/).
